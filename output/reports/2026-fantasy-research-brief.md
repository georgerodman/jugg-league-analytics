## Methodology

Player votes are deduplicated by analyst, using the newest retained opinion for the deterministic positive/negative split. Target, Avoid, and Watch are normalized action signals; Sleeper, Breakout, Value, and Bust are descriptive tags. Card summaries and full writeups are generated only from retained takeaways and cached by an input hash and prompt version. Team-level articles provide context but do not create player votes. Manual overrides in the application affect the displayed flag only.
